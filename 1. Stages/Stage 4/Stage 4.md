# Stage 4 — The benchmark. Matched filtering, at equal false-alarm rate.

**Machine:** 🖥️ **Desktop PC**, inside WSL2 — this stage is CPU work (16 threads eat the
template bank); the GPU only reprises the CNN forward passes
**Depends on:** [[Stage 2]] ✅ (its conditioned crops + val/test splits), [[Stage 3]] ✅
(its glitch test set + both CNN checkpoints)
**Previous:** [[Stage 3]] ✅

> **The goal:** the fight the whole project was built to referee. `pycbc`-style matched
> filtering vs the CNNs, **compared at equal false-alarm probability** — never at equal
> accuracy — on three fronts:
> 1. **Efficiency** on real-noise injections (Stage 2 test) — where MF's optimality
>    theorem holds *approximately* (the bulk is quasi-Gaussian) and the CNN should lose;
> 2. **Glitch robustness** (Stage 3 glitch test) — where the theorem's Gaussian
>    assumption dies, and the CNN has its shot;
> 3. **Speed** — one forward pass vs a whole template bank, with respectable hardware
>    on both sides (3070 vs 3700X×16).

## 1. Decisions pinned before writing code

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **The arms** | **C** = Stage-2 CNN, **D** = Stage-3 CNN, **E** = MF max-SNR ρ over a template bank, **F** = MF ρ̃ (ρ re-weighted by the power χ², the "newSNR" real searches actually threshold on), plus an **oracle** curve (true-parameter template per injection) on the efficiency plot only | Bare max-SNR MF loses to glitches *by construction* — beating it would be beating a strawman. F is the real method. The oracle is MF's ceiling and this stage's fraud alarm. |
| **What MF sees** | The **same conditioned crops** the CNNs see — per-block causally whitened, bandpassed, ±20σ saturated (Stage 3's contract), read through the same Dataset classes | Equal inputs or the comparison means nothing. In the whitened domain a correlation *is* the matched filter, and the saturation only touches glitch rows — where it *helps* MF (a 6,000σ glitch would otherwise dominate its own ρ). Said out loud: MF gets the same per-block PSD knowledge the conditioning pipeline has — no more, no less. |
| **Templates** | IMRPhenomD, m1 ≥ m2 ∈ [10,50] M☉, nonspinning — generated by `make_waveform` → `place_in_buffer` → the block's own conditioning, **the identical pipeline the injections went through** | The crop holds only the final ~1 s of a waveform that can run 3+ s from 30 Hz. Templates built any other way would integrate SNR the crop doesn't contain, and the efficiency x-axis would lie. |
| **The bank** | **Stochastic placement at minimal match 0.97**, matches computed on crop-truncated templates under a reference train-block whitening filter; seeded, deterministic; stop at 500 consecutive rejections | Standard practice, small enough to build in minutes; the check measures effectualness on held-out draws *and* under other blocks' filters. Reference filter comes from the **earliest train block** — the bank never touches val/test noise. |
| **Detection statistic** | Per crop: ρ = max over templates × lags × phase (quadrature via the whitened template's Hilbert pair) of the normalized correlation; χ² = 8-bin power chi-squared at the peak; ρ̃ = newSNR(ρ, χ²ᵣ) | Textbook. Phase max via analytic signal is exact for a dominant-mode waveform. 8 bins is plenty for a 1 s crop in a 30–350 Hz band. |
| **Thresholds** | Per arm, FAP 1e-2 / 1e-3 quantiles of the arm's own scores on **Stage 2 val negatives** — ordinary real noise, identical protocol to Stage 3's eval | "Equal false-alarm rate" is the whole point of the stage; the protocol is already field-tested. |
| **Speed protocol** | Report **latency** (batch-1, single crop end-to-end) and **throughput** (best-batch) separately, for E/F (16 threads, cached per-block templates) and C/D (GPU, and CPU batch-1 for honesty) | Batch amortization masquerading as latency is the classic benchmark sin. LIGO cares about latency. |
| **Scores are cached** | Every arm's score for every row of s2-val / s2-test / s3-test → `~/ligo-data/stage4_scores.h5`, written once by `stage4_mf.py` | Eval then iterates in seconds; reruns are bit-identical (seeded); the check script interrogates the same file the eval reads. |

> [!warning] 🐛 The bandlimited-noise trap — one number, three hats
> First scoring run: noise quadrature std **1.719**, oracle recovery **1.696**, χ²ᵣ on
> matched injections **2.98 ≈ 1.7²**. One cause: conditioned noise has unit variance but
> is NOT white — its power sits in the 30–350 Hz band at ~3.2× the white spectral level,
> exactly where the templates live (√3.2 ≈ 1.8). A flat-metric correlation is not the
> matched filter on bandlimited noise. Fix: **re-whiten per block, causally** — the
> conditioned-noise spectrum is estimated from 64 crops of the block's role-0 PSD-source
> predecessor (never scored, never trained on), all spectra weighted by 1/√S in-band.
> Relative statistics barely moved (bank/oracle was 0.997 both times) — but every
> absolute threshold and the χ² normalization were wearing the same wrong constant.

> [!warning] 🐛 A pooled variance is one glitch away from nonsense
> After re-whitening, arms E/F/O all plateaued at ~0.95 efficiency in EVERY SNR bin —
> ~5% of even SNR-18 injections "missed". The eval's own tell flagged it (CNN beating
> the oracle by +0.054: theorem violation ⇒ bug). Diagnosis: the badly-lost rows had
> ρ_oracle down to 0.05×SNR — **below the max-over-4096-lags noise floor (~4), which is
> impossible** unless the whole block was deflated. The per-block calibration scalar s0
> pooled *variances* over its 64 calibration crops; one loud glitch in a PSD-source
> block (Stage 2 measured crop stds up to 263) inflates a pooled variance ~30× and
> silently crushes every ρ in that block — one bad block ≈ the uniform 5%. Fix: s0 =
> **median of per-crop stds** — the same medicine the PSD estimate already takes.
> The check that "passed" before pooled over all val blocks and diluted it; the eval's
> theorem-tell is what actually caught it.

**Front 1 — the theorem's home turf (efficiency, s2-test).** At FAP 1e-2 the matched
filter is optimal like it's supposed to be: E and the oracle hit **1.000** in every bin
at SNR ≥ 8 (CNNs 0.986–1.000, always ≤ oracle); at 6–8 MF wins outright (0.931 vs
0.887/0.890). At the ragged 4–6 edge the CNN noses ahead (C 0.465 vs O 0.376) — the
bank's 62 trials and the CNN's freedom both out-fire a single template there; that is
trials-factor physics at the noise floor, not a theorem crack.

**Front 1b — and then FAP 1e-3 happens.** Arm E is **functionally blind** (efficiency
0.000–0.006 everywhere): on real noise its 1e-3 threshold is set by the unlabelled-
glitch tail of the val negatives — Stage 2's discovery, now measured for matched
filtering. The χ² veto is what gives matched filtering its 1e-3 regime back: arm F sits
at 0.85–0.999 — and **beats both CNNs at every SNR at 1e-3** (0.850 vs 0.720/0.658 at
6–8). The theorem's fine print was always "Gaussian noise"; on real data the veto is
load-bearing.

**Front 2 — the theorem's graveyard (glitches, s3-test).** ALL-GLITCH false-alarm
fraction:

| arm | @1e-2 | @1e-3 |
|---|---|---|
| E — MF max-ρ | 0.582 | 0.116 |
| C — CNN glitch-naive | 0.331 | 0.159 |
| D — CNN glitch-trained | 0.166 | 0.050 |
| **F — MF newSNR** | **0.100** | **0.047** |

Bare max-SNR MF is exactly the strawman the plan warned about — it fires on 100% of
Koi_Fish and Tomte at 1e-2. The honest fight is D vs F: **F edges D overall** (0.100 vs
0.166 @1e-2; 0.047 vs 0.050 @1e-3 — a dead heat), with real complementarity per class:
the χ² annihilates Koi_Fish (0.000 @1e-3 vs D's 0.102) because loud-and-mismatched is
its home case, while D handles Extremely_Loud better (0.109 vs 0.145 @1e-3) and they
tie on Whistle (0.095 each). The glitch-trained CNN learned, from 1,020 labelled
specimens, roughly what the χ² veto encodes analytically — arm D beats arm E everywhere
— but it did not surpass the engineered statistic.

**Front 3 — speed.** MF: 8.2 ms/crop (62 templates, warm bank, 1 core) + 2.7 ms/crop
amortized re-conditioning; 42 crops/s through the full 16-thread pipeline (oracle
included). CNN: **0.47 ms/crop on a single CPU core** (the 251k-param model is so small
the GPU only pays off in batch: 0.81 ms batch-1, **158,000 crops/s at batch 256**).
Latency ~20×, throughput ~3,700× — and the CNN needs no template bank, no χ² binning,
no per-block re-conditioning at inference.

### ✅ Step 4 — Notes + commit

## The verdict, in terms of the two assumptions

1. **Gaussianity.** Where the noise is quasi-Gaussian (bulk, FAP 1e-2), the theorem
   held to the decimal: nothing beat the oracle, anywhere (the tell read +0.000). Where
   the noise's non-Gaussian tail rules (FAP 1e-3, glitch-rich data), *naked* matched
   filtering collapsed — blind on injections, 58% glitch false-alarm — and was rescued
   only by an engineered statistic (χ²) that is itself a response to the assumption
   failing. The CNN learned an equivalent rescue from data alone (D ≈ F on glitch
   rejection at 1e-3), which is the project's thesis demonstrated: the gap MF's theorem
   leaves on real noise is learnable structure.
2. **Template coverage.** Untested here by design — every injection was drawn from the
   same family the bank covers (FF ≥ 0.97). The CNN's generalization edge, if any,
   lives outside this benchmark (a follow-on: score waveforms *off* the bank manifold).
3. **The practical margin is speed**, and it is enormous (20× latency, 3,700×
   throughput on respectable hardware both sides) — which is exactly why the field
   cares about learned detectors for low-latency alerts, with the matched filter
   remaining the offline gold standard.

## 3. Footguns

### ⚠️⚠️ The strawman MF
Max-SNR-only MF fires on every loud transient — comparing the glitch-trained CNN against
it and declaring victory would be dishonest. Arm F (χ²-reweighted) is not optional; it is
the real method, and per-class results must be read against *it*. If D beats E but loses
to F, that is the honest headline.

### ⚠️ The crop truncation asymmetry
A 10+10 M☉ waveform runs ~3 s from 30 Hz; the crop keeps the last ~1 s. Both the
injections (Stage 1's `place`) and the templates (same call chain) must truncate
identically. A full-length template would claim SNR the data never contained.

### ⚠️ Whitening residue vs the correlation normalization
Conditioned crops are only *approximately* unit-PSD in-band (truncated whitening filter,
FIR shoulders). The noise-calibration check measures the per-template σ empirically on
train-side negatives; if it's off by >10%, normalize per-template with the measured σ —
still causal, still leak-free.

### ⚠️ Bank knowledge boundaries
The bank's reference filter and any empirical normalization come from **train blocks
only**. MF may use each row's own block PSD — the conditioning pipeline already grants
exactly that knowledge to both classes of arm.

### 🚩 The tell, Stage 4 edition — now pointing the other way
If a CNN beats the **oracle** matched filter on Stage-2-test efficiency anywhere at
SNR ≥ 8, that is not a triumph — it is a **theorem violation on the quasi-Gaussian bulk**
and means a bug (leakage into the CNNs, or a broken MF normalization mislabelling the
axis). The CNN is only *allowed* to win on the glitch table, and modestly at the ragged
low-SNR edge where "Gaussian" is least true.
